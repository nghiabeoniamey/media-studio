## Gaps flagged by the verifier

- CRITICAL (new in the last 3 months): all Veo 3.1 models on the Gemini API (veo-3.1-generate-preview, -fast-generate-preview, -lite-generate-preview) are 'Preview' and list a shutdown date of Oct 22, 2026, about 13 days from now, with gemini-omni-1.1-flash as the recommended replacement (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/deprecations). The report's 'Veo 3.1 Lite $0.05/s on the Gemini API' low-cost pick is about to disappear. The Google replacement on that API, Omni 1.1 Flash, costs about $0.10/s at 720p (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing). Veo continues only on Vertex: Standard/Fast GA retire 'Nov 17, 2026 or later'. Lite (veo-3.1-lite-generate-001) is still Preview, us-central1 only, and its modalities row says image input 'Not supported' while its capabilities row says image-to-video is supported. Test image-to-video on Vertex Lite before building on it (https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/veo/3-1-generate).
- Budget scope: the brief says 2-3 videos/day PER NICHE with <$100/month TOTAL AI cost, but the report's budget table covers a single niche (60-90 videos). Adding a second niche (kittens) doubles generation cost. The architecture should enforce a per-niche monthly cap and per-video motion-second budget.
- Runway's API works as a near-list-price aggregator: H3 Max at $0.05/$0.08, Wan 3.0 at $0.05/$0.10/$0.20, Omni 1.1 at $0.034-$0.30, and Grok 1.5 Lite at $0.02/$0.03 plus 1 credit per start frame, with credits at $0.01 (https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/pricing/). fal charges about 2x BytePlus for Seedance 2.5 ($21.4/M vs $10.70/M tokens). The report's 'use fal as the single aggregator' advice should be weighed against Runway, or direct vendor APIs, on cost.
- Kling direct API is not only a $700 minimum: Kling documents a Trial Resource Package, which third parties put at $9.80 per 100 units for 30 days (https://kling.ai/document-api/guides/get-started/quick-start, https://www.eesel.ai/blog/kling-ai-pricing). Units expire after 180 days on standard packages.
- Hidden per-input costs that matter for character consistency: MiniMax H3 Max charges $0.074 per reference image beyond 2 and $0.143/s for 768P reference video (https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/pricing-paygo); xAI charges $0.01 per input image (https://docs.x.ai/developers/pricing); Seedance bills input reference-video seconds; Agnes bills input video seconds at the output rate.
- Failure and moderation billing differs by vendor, which matters for the retry factor. Veo does not charge blocked generations (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/veo). xAI charges for violating generations, plus $0.05 for pre-generation rejections in the Responses API (https://docs.x.ai/developers/pricing). BytePlus reportedly does not charge moderation failures (third-party, via the CometAPI/Cellcog summaries). Vidu reportedly bills failed takes (third-party). For a religious niche likely to trip filters (crucifixion violence, infants), this changes the effective price.
