## Gaps flagged by the verifier

- Kitten niche: YouTube's official monetization page now lists 'Unsatisfying or Off-putting Content' (July 2026). Its non-monetizable examples include 'a series showing repetitive scenarios of animals in exaggerated distress or peril'. 'Generic or Repetitive Content' also names 'videos where characters are put in the same situation over and over again with the same outcome'. Story templates for the kitten niche must vary the plot and outcome from video to video. Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392, https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/
- AI disclosure labels: YouTube requires the 'AI use' disclosure for content that 'generates a realistic scene that didn't actually occur', for AI music that is the main focus, and when a real person appears to say or do something they didn't. 'Cinematic realistic' Bible reenactments and the biography niche will probably need the label; Pixar-style or illustrated content probably won't. Gemini images and Lyria audio carry SynthID, and OpenAI images carry C2PA. TikTok auto-labels from C2PA (third-party reports). Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- Facebook originality update (Mar 13, 2026): unoriginal content is deprioritized, and Pages that keep posting mostly unoriginal content can be made non-recommendable and demonetized. 'Narrating what's already on screen without adding anything meaningful' counts as unoriginal. This matters for the target 'Jesus Daily'-style FB pages. Source: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/
- Made-for-kids risk: 3D Pixar-style kitten videos may be classified 'made for kids'. YouTube's kids quality principles then apply; one of them, 'Hard to follow', is described as 'often the result of mass production or autogeneration'. Made-for-kids status also brings COPPA ad limits (not verified this session), which would cut revenue a lot. Decide the audience designation before building the niche. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- Bible pipeline: API.Bible's Oct 1, 2026 FAQ forbids sending copyrighted Bible text to LLMs as prompt context and forbids AI text-to-speech of it. Keep every LLM and TTS step on public-domain texts. The Berean Standard Bible (BSB) has been public domain since Apr 30, 2023 and is a modern-English option the report missed; it has 66 books only, so deuterocanonical stories still need WEBC or DRA. Sources: https://care.api.bible/article/405-express-licensing-faqs, https://berean.bible/terms.htm
- Model lifecycle dates the report missed. Gemini TTS previews (3.1 Flash TTS preview, 2.5 Flash/Pro preview TTS) shut down Nov 17, 2026. Veo 3.1 preview models and gemini-omni-flash-preview shut down Oct 22, 2026 (matters to the video-generation research). OpenAI announced on Aug 26, 2026 that whisper-1, gpt-4o-transcribe and gpt-4o-mini-transcribe shut down Feb 26, 2027; the report lists gpt-4o-mini-transcribe as a caption option without flagging this. Nano Banana 2 has no official shutdown date, despite third-party claims of Oct 29. Sources: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/deprecations, https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog

Gemini lists a 'Flex' tier at Batch prices for 3.8 Flash, 3.8 Flash TTS and Nano Banana Pro (not for NB 2.1); it is worth testing as a cheaper option without the 24-hour wait.
